Scaling Laravel Apps with Redis Caching

Practical patterns for caching database queries, sessions, and queues to handle high traffic without burning your DB.

Redis isn't just a key-value store — used well, it becomes the shock absorber between your users and your database. In this article I walk through the cache topology I use on production Laravel projects: a read-through cache for expensive queries, stamped cache keys with version tags so invalidation stays simple, and separate stores for sessions and queues so one saturated queue never evicts hot data.
